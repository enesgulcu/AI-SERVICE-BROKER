# Compliance Approval Checklist

Status: Pre-production gate  
This checklist organizes required specialist review; it is not legal advice.

## Lead acquisition and first contact

- [ ] Each lead source has a documented acquisition method, platform-terms
      assessment, lawful basis, evidence owner and expiry/review date.
- [ ] Phone/WhatsApp outreach has written 6563/IYS analysis or documented
      exception and proof requirements.
- [ ] Opt-out keywords, suppression list, wrong-number handling and evidence
      retention are approved and tested.
- [ ] Scraping or third-party resale is disabled unless explicitly approved.

## KVKK and data lifecycle

- [ ] Data controller/processor roles and processing inventory are approved.
- [ ] Notice timing/content and processing conditions are defined per purpose.
- [ ] Retention/destruction schedule covers raw leads, messages, summaries,
      requirements, risk records, prompts, logs, analytics, backups and DLQs.
- [ ] Access, correction, deletion and objection request procedures are tested.
- [ ] Meta, AI, cloud, monitoring and analytics subprocessors plus cross-border
      transfer mechanisms are approved.
- [ ] Special-category, child and third-party data have explicit allow/deny,
      minimization, quarantine and incident rules.

## Commercial and employment role

- [ ] The company's actual intermediary/employer/service-provider role and any
      licensing requirements are confirmed by qualified counsel.
- [ ] Approved/restricted terminology and liability language are versioned.
- [ ] Quote/acceptance, pre-contract information, tax/VAT, cancellation,
      withdrawal, refund, replacement and dispute semantics are approved.
- [ ] Worker age, sex, nationality, language, appearance and similar preference
      policies have employment/discrimination review.

## AI and automated decisions

- [ ] Customer-facing AI disclosure and route to a human are approved.
- [ ] Model training, retention, region and subprocessor terms are approved.
- [ ] Risk enforcement has human review/appeal and fairness monitoring.
- [ ] No solely automated punitive or legally significant action is enabled
      without explicit legal and policy authorization.

## WhatsApp

- [ ] WABA ownership, opt-in wording/evidence and approved template categories
      are documented.
- [ ] 24-hour customer-service window, re-engagement, rate/quality controls and
      account-suspension response are implemented.
- [ ] Unsubscribe atomically cancels pending follow-ups and outbound messages.

## Approval record

Every checked item must link to the approver, date, jurisdiction/scope,
effective version, evidence and next review date. Approval artifacts must not
contain unnecessary personal data.
